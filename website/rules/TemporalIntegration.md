This document explains how data may be aggregated between elections.

All integration is *nominal* not *spatial*. It is the user's responsibility
to decide if the geography of interest has remained substantially the same
between the elections of interest.

Nominal integration may be used for counties, municipalities, and minor civil
divisions (municipalities within counties). Nominal integration may also be used
for state legislative and congressional districts. Reporting units shall not be
integrated between elections, even when they bear the same name.
